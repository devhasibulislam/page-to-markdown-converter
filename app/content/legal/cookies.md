---
title: "Cookie policy"
updated: 2026-08-19
---

MarkDrop does not set any cookies. Not first-party, not third-party, not
essential, not analytics. Nothing.

## What that means

- No consent banner is required
- Your browser will not receive any `Set-Cookie` headers from our servers
- We cannot recognize you between visits by cookie
- We have no cookie preferences to save because there are no cookies

## What about the browser extension?

The extension uses `chrome.storage` (or the equivalent WebExtension API) to
remember your last-selected delivery method and the backend URL. That data
lives inside your browser and is never sent to us.

## If this ever changes

If we ever add analytics or session cookies, this page will be updated first
and a cookie consent banner will appear at the same time.
